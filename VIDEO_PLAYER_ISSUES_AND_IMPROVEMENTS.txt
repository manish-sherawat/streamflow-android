================================================================================
       STREAMFLOW ANDROID - VIDEO PLAYER ISSUES & AUDIT FINDINGS
================================================================================

This document details identified technical issues, UX gaps, and edge cases
found within the Video Player implementation (PlayerScreen.kt, PlayerViewModel.kt,
PlayerControlsSheet.kt).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CONTROLS AUTO-HIDE & OVERLAY CONFLICTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[FIXED] Top Bar Auto-Hide:
        - Implemented 3.5s auto-hide timer for top/bottom player control bars.
        - Single tap anywhere on screen smoothly toggles overlay visibility with fadeIn()/fadeOut().
[FIXED] Gesture & Controller Handling:
        - Touch interactions automatically reset the auto-hide timer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AUDIO FOCUS & MEDIA LIFECYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[FIXED] ExoPlayer Audio Focus Handling:
        - Configured ExoPlayer with C.USAGE_MEDIA and C.AUDIO_CONTENT_TYPE_MOVIE AudioAttributes
          with setHandleAudioAttributes(audioAttributes, true).
[FIXED] Clean Lifecycle Management:
        - On ON_PAUSE or disposal, progress is synchronized and player is properly released.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ORIENTATION & ASPECT RATIO CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[FIXED] Aspect Ratio Switcher:
        - Added Aspect Ratio button (Icons.Filled.AspectRatio) to the top overlay.
        - Cycles dynamically between Fit (16:9), Crop to Fill, and Stretch modes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ERROR RECOVERY & NETWORK DROPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[FIXED] Glassmorphic Error Retry Card:
        - Created a glassmorphic error overlay with a "Retry Connection" button that calls retryPlayback().

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SUBTITLE & TRACK CUSTOMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[FIXED] Subtitle Appearance Controls:
        - Added Font Size (Small, Medium, Large) and Text Color (White, Yellow, Cyan) selectors
          in PlayerControlsSheet and bound them to PlayerView's SubtitleView.
================================================================================
