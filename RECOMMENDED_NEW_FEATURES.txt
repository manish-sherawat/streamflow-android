================================================================================
       STREAMFLOW ANDROID - PROPOSED & RECOMMENDED FUTURE FEATURES
================================================================================

This document outlines remaining high-impact feature recommendations 
for StreamFlow Android across all app screens and the Video Player.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOME SCREEN (HomeScreen.kt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Auto-Playing Silent Video Previews:
    - Hero banner automatically previews a 10-second silent trailer loop when focused.
[ ] "Coming Soon" & Remind Me Pipeline:
    - Upcoming releases rail with a "Remind Me" bell button to schedule push notifications.
[ ] Personalized "Because You Watched X":
    - Recommendation rails tailored dynamically to user watch history.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TITLE DETAIL SCREEN (TitleDetailScreen.kt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] In-App Official Trailer Player Modal:
    - Tapping "Watch Trailer" plays the trailer in a floating video dialog.
[ ] Season Bulk Download Button:
    - Ability to download entire seasons with one tap ("Download Season 1").

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SEARCH SCREEN (SearchScreen.kt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Voice Search (Speech-to-Text):
    - Microphone button on search bar allowing voice queries.
[ ] Advanced Multi-Filters:
    - Filter matrix combining Content Rating (PG-13, R, TV-MA), Language, and Studio.
[ ] Popular Tag Cloud:
    - Interactive trending search tags (#Marvel, #SciFi, #TopAnime, #Cyberpunk).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WATCHLIST / MY LIST SCREEN (WatchlistScreen.kt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Custom Folders & Playlists:
    - Ability to organize saved titles into custom lists (e.g. "Anime Favorites", "Weekend Binge").
[ ] Watch Status Indicators:
    - Visual badges for "Unwatched", "In Progress", and "Completed".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PROFILE & ACCOUNT SCREEN (ProfileScreen.kt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Multi-User Profile Management & Kids Mode:
    - Avatar creation, PIN protection for adult profiles, and curated Kids profile mode.
[ ] Download Manager & Storage Settings:
    - Management screen for downloaded videos with SD card / internal storage path selector.
